# Campaigns, incentive mechanisms and branding practices for getting AED owners to register and share device data (state as of 21 Nov 2026)

Provenance legend used below: **[read]** = page/PDF opened and read this session; **[snippet]** = taken from a search-result summary, page itself not opened (verify before quoting verbatim). Local task documents are cited by file path.

## 1. Case studies with numbers: which campaigns raised AED registration, and how (names, slogans, identities, mechanics, results)

### Takeaway
The registries that grew fastest combine (a) one named national "network" wired into ambulance dispatch, (b) a recurring, time-boxed public push with a numeric target (BHF "125,000 by year end", Défib'Action "30,000 confirmed in a week", PulsePoint's October contest), and (c) a concrete reward or recognition loop (badges, prizes of AEDs, press coverage). A legal duty alone (France) left the state register roughly a third the size of the NGO/crowd database.

### Cited Findings

**UK — British Heart Foundation, "The Circuit: the national defibrillator network"**
- Registered devices: "over 110,000" as of 2025 [snippet] — [BHF network page](https://www.bhf.org.uk/how-you-can-help/how-to-save-a-life/defibrillators/national-defibrillator-network-the-circuit); "120,000+" on 22 Jul 2026 [read] — [BHF, defibrillator deserts 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/july/uk-defibrillator-deserts-2026); "just under 123,000" on 10 Aug 2026 with a public target of 125,000 by end of 2026 [read] — [BHF, 10 most extraordinary defibrillators](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/august/ten-most-extraordinary-defibrillators-the-circuit); 124,000 in Oct 2026 [snippet] — [BHF, Restart a Heart Day 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/october/restart-a-heart-day-2026-building-a-nation-of-lifesavers).
- Still incomplete: "tens of thousands of defibrillators are not yet registered" [snippet] — [Resuscitation Council UK, The Circuit](https://www.resus.org.uk/public-resource/defibrillation/circuit).
- Partners: developed by BHF with Resuscitation Council UK, St John Ambulance, NHS England, the Association of Ambulance Chief Executives and Save a Life Cymru; funded in the past year by Omaze; data used by NHS ambulance services [read] — [BHF Aug 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/august/ten-most-extraordinary-defibrillators-the-circuit).
- Core message to owners (CEO quote): unregistered devices "are not visible to ambulance services in an emergency, when seconds count"; "Please help us find these hidden devices. It's easy to register, and it could save a life." Section heading: "Help us reach a new milestone" [read] — [BHF Aug 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/august/ten-most-extraordinary-defibrillators-the-circuit).
- PR mechanic Aug 2026: photo story of the "10 most extraordinary defibrillators" (Snowdon summit, a pub at 1,732 ft, one 9 m below sea level, islands with 4–85 residents, one Guardian who looks after 182 devices) used as the hook for the registration ask [read] — [BHF Aug 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/august/ten-most-extraordinary-defibrillators-the-circuit).
- Role naming: registrants are called "Guardians"; they must create an account before registering and then get automated emails every 3 months prompting them to update the record [snippet] — [Resuscitation Plus 2024, PMC11382004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11382004).
- Evaluation: The Circuit was rolled out to all 14 UK ambulance services, and 54% of AEDs on it had not previously been registered with any ambulance service [snippet] — [Resuscitation Plus, Sep 2024 (DOAJ record)](https://doaj.org/article/55a79a00c84a4253b9810615a90cc88d).
- Friction claim used by ambulance trusts: registration "takes less than five minutes" and is free [snippet] — [South Central Ambulance Service](https://www.scas.nhs.uk/scas-urges-local-schools-to-register-defibrillators-on-the-circuit/).
- Coverage-gap campaign (22 Jul 2026): BHF named 10 "defibrillator deserts" (e.g. Barking and Dagenham, Liverpool, Southampton, Nottingham, Wrexham, Newport, West Tyrone) chosen by long retrieval times, high population density and high deprivation; longest average retrieval time 19 min 20 s (rural West Tyrone); 400 funded packages offered via the relaunched Community Defibrillator Funding Programme (1,000 devices placed so far), with free replacement parts for up to 10 years; every awarded device is registered on The Circuit; corporate partners Royal Mail, Sky Bet, Omaze; key line "every second counts" [read] — [BHF Jul 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/july/uk-defibrillator-deserts-2026).
- Sector-specific relays exist (ambulance trust to schools; a county sports partnership under the headline "Keep Hearts Beating – Get defibrillators registered on The Circuit") [snippet] — [SCAS](https://www.scas.nhs.uk/scas-urges-local-schools-to-register-defibrillators-on-the-circuit/); [Yorkshire Sport](https://www.yorkshiresport.org/whats-new/news/keep-hearts-beating-get-defibrillators-registered-on-the-circuit).

**Denmark — TrygFonden "Hjertestarter-Netværk" (hjertestarter.dk) and "Hjerteløber"**

- A dedicated owner flyer "Registrer din hjertestarter" exists, and regional TV publishes per-municipality AED counts ("Se listen: Så mange hjertestartere er der i din kommune") [snippet, titles only] — [flyer](https://edit.hjertestarter.dk/-/media/downloads/ny-flyer--register-din-hjertestarter.pdf); [TV 2 Kosmopol](https://www.tv2kosmopol.dk/metropolen/se-listen-saa-mange-hjertestartere-er-der-i-din-kommune).

**France — Géo'DAE (state register) and "Défib'Action" (Staying Alive citizen campaign)**
- Law no. 2018-527 of 28 Jun 2018 obliges certain public-access establishments to install an AED and created the national database Géo'DAE, run by the Direction Générale de la Santé; owners must declare devices and may delegate declaration to their maintenance company [snippet] — [Assemblée nationale, QE 2841](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/questions/QANR5L16QE2841.pdf).
- 105,250 AEDs declared since the base opened in March 2020 (date of the figure not given) [snippet] — [Assemblée nationale, QE 11010](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/questions/QANR5L16QE11010.pdf); national fleet estimated at about 300,000 by market studies of June 2021 [snippet] — [Assemblée nationale, QE 10753](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/questions/QANR5L16QE10753.pdf).
- Géo'DAE is published as open data on data.gouv.fr, validated by owners and continuously updated [snippet] — [data.gouv.fr dataset](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/geodae-base-nationale-des-defibrillateurs).
- Défib'Action 2026 (press release dated 8 Jun 2026) [read] — [Staying Alive CP](https://stayingalive.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CP-DefibAction-2026.pdf):
  - 4th annual edition, one week (20–28 Jun 2026); headline "La chasse aux défibrillateurs est ouverte !"; tagline "Tous mobilisés face à l'arrêt cardiaque".
  - Four allowed actions in the app: add a missing AED, confirm an existing one, enrich a record (photos, opening hours), report a problem.
  - Record of 20,513 AEDs confirmed in the 2025 edition; 2026 target 30,000 confirmed.
  - New in 2026: collectible badges per action type (added / modified / confirmed / reported) and per place visited; president's framing: gamification "c'est une façon de dire merci".
  - Staying Alive database: 290,000 AEDs in France, +21% in one year; 500,000 citizen responders; used by emergency services in 91 départements.
  - Institutional backing: Direction Générale de la Santé is partner since the 3rd edition, explicitly because the campaign "permettra de rendre plus fiables les données référencées dans Géo'DAE"; relayed by the national firefighters' federation, the Paris fire brigade and civil-protection associations.
  - Visual identity observed in the PDF: handwritten script wordmark "Défib'Action" with an orange heart replacing the "o"; Staying Alive mark = green heart with white lightning bolt plus two-tone wordmark; Géo'DAE logo = blue map pin with a red ECG line and tricolour dot, caption "Base nationale des défibrillateurs"; document typeface Lexend (PDF metadata).

**USA — MyHeartMap Challenge (University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, 2012)**
- Prize design: USD 10,000 grand prize for the person/team reporting the most AEDs (conditional on at least 750 AEDs reported overall or 500 by one team) plus USD 50 for photographing each of 20–200 pre-identified "Golden" AEDs; modelled on the DARPA Network Challenge; ran 31 Jan–13 Mar 2012 (the page also gives 27 Mar 2012 as the official end; unreconciled) [read] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MyHeartMap_Challenge).
- Result: over 1,500 AEDs submitted by more than 300 teams and individuals [read] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MyHeartMap_Challenge); in more than 800 buildings, mostly offices, gyms/recreation centres and schools [snippet] — [Penn Medicine release](https://www.pennmedicine.org/news/news-releases/2012/may/two-winners-take-grand-prize-i).
- Two winners each found more than 400 AEDs and each received USD 9,000; they won by walking buildings in person rather than by online crowdsourcing; searchers often met "blank stares, red tape, or mistrust" from building staff; the prior estimate of 5,000 public AEDs was revised to about 2,500 [snippet] — [Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/uncategorized/myheartmap-challenge-ends-more-than-1100-aeds-found/); [Penn Medicine release](https://www.pennmedicine.org/news/news-releases/2012/may/two-winners-take-grand-prize-i).
- Peer-reviewed write-up: Merchant RM, Asch DA, Hershey JC et al., "A Crowdsourcing Innovation Challenge to Locate and Map Automated External Defibrillators", Circ Cardiovasc Qual Outcomes 2013;6:229–236 [snippet; abstract not retrievable this session] — [PubMed 23481522](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23481522/).

### Inferences
- The recurring pattern is "named network + numeric milestone + time box": BHF's 125,000 target, Défib'Action's one week and 30,000 target, PulsePoint's October window. A Polish action should have a countable goal (e.g. number of providers or records on dane.gov.pl by a date) so the slogan and logo can be attached to a visible counter.
- France shows a mandate without a campaign under-delivers: about 105k declared in Géo'DAE versus 290k in the Staying Alive crowd/NGO database and a ~300k market estimate, and the health ministry now partners with the NGO campaign to fix its own register. For Poland (no mandate found, see section 3) recognition and low friction have to carry the load.
- MyHeartMap shows cash prizes buy a one-off census (two people found over half the devices) but do not create owner-maintained data; BHF's "Guardian" role plus quarterly reminders is the model that addresses maintenance. The Polish challenge targets institutional data holders, which is closer to The Circuit/Géo'DAE than to MyHeartMap.
- The 54% "previously unknown to ambulance services" figure is the single strongest proof point for the pitch: a unified register reveals devices that already exist.
- Piggybacking on a large existing audience (Pokémon GO, Yanosik, Ninja Van, Royal Mail) is a repeated distribution tactic; the Polish equivalent would be existing institutional channels (dane.gov.pl provider base, municipal associations).

## 2. Behavioural levers that work on institutional data holders

### Takeaway
Evidence is strongest (though mostly descriptive, not experimental) for four levers: making the ask tiny and concrete ("under five minutes", one short URL), giving the holder a named role and public recognition, scheduled reminders, and showing local gaps or rankings. No controlled trial comparing incentives for AED registration by institutions was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Friction reduction:** "less than five minutes" and free is the headline claim relayed by ambulance trusts for The Circuit [snippet] — [SCAS](https://www.scas.nhs.uk/scas-urges-local-schools-to-register-defibrillators-on-the-circuit/); PulsePoint uses a memorable short URL, AED.new [snippet] — [PulsePoint](https://www.pulsepoint.org/aedcontest); BHF's CEO line: "It's easy to register" [read] — [BHF Aug 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/august/ten-most-extraordinary-defibrillators-the-circuit).
- **Delegation for multi-site owners:** in France the operator can have its maintenance company declare and update devices in Géo'DAE on its behalf [snippet] — [Assemblée nationale, QE 2841](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/questions/QANR5L16QE2841.pdf); BHF publishes an organisation-administrator guide for The Circuit [snippet, title only] — [BHF org admin guide](https://www.bhf.org.uk/orgadminguide).
- **Role identity:** owners are "Guardians" (UK) and are publicly thanked for keeping devices "rescue-ready"; individual Guardians are profiled in national press material [read] — [BHF Aug 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/august/ten-most-extraordinary-defibrillators-the-circuit).
- **Reminders:** automated email every 3 months asking Guardians to update the record [snippet] — [PMC11382004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11382004).
- **Certification/recognition:** Taiwan's "AED Safe Place" certificate requires registration in the national database as a precondition, bundling data sharing with a visible status (5,820 certified places reported for 2022) [snippet] — [Taichung Health Bureau](https://www.health.taichung.gov.tw/media/1046442/安心場所認證流程11304.pdf); [MOHW 2023 report](https://service.mohw.gov.tw/ebook/dopl/112/02/files/basic-html/page52.html). A US "HEARTsafe" community (Eugene Springfield) won the 2023 PulsePoint contest [snippet] — [PulsePoint HEARTsafe tag](https://pulsepoint.org/news/tag/heartsafe).
- **Competition between communities:** PulsePoint's contest ranks communities by AEDs registered in one month and rewards the winner with AEDs (2025: Anchorage, 132 AEDs registered) [snippet] — [PulsePoint 2025](https://www.pulsepoint.org/news/anchorage-alaska-wins-2025-pulsepoint-aed-contest). Danish regional TV publishes per-municipality AED counts [snippet, title] — [TV 2 Kosmopol](https://www.tv2kosmopol.dk/metropolen/se-listen-saa-mange-hjertestartere-er-der-i-din-kommune).
- **Badges for every action, including verification:** Défib'Action 2026 rewards add/modify/confirm/report separately; its predecessor edition without badges already reached 20,513 confirmations in a week [read] — [Staying Alive CP](https://stayingalive.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CP-DefibAction-2026.pdf).
- **Showing local coverage gaps:** BHF's named "defibrillator deserts" with a retrieval-time metric (19 min 20 s worst case) paired with an immediate offer (400 funded packages) [read] — [BHF Jul 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/july/uk-defibrillator-deserts-2026).
- **Conditionality:** every BHF-funded device must be registered on The Circuit [read] — [BHF Jul 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/july/uk-defibrillator-deserts-2026).
- **Legal nudge and its limits:** France's 2018 law created a declaration duty, yet about 105,250 declarations compare with a market estimate of about 300,000 devices [snippet] — [QE 11010](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/questions/QANR5L16QE11010.pdf); [QE 10753](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/questions/QANR5L16QE10753.pdf).
- **Loss framing over liability framing:** the dominant message in the successful campaigns is "your device is invisible/hidden to the ambulance service" and "every second/minute counts", not legal risk [read] — [BHF Aug 2026](https://www.bhf.org.uk/what-we-do/news-from-the-bhf/news-archive/2026/august/ten-most-extraordinary-defibrillators-the-circuit); [Staying Alive CP](https://stayingalive.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CP-DefibAction-2026.pdf).
- **Gatekeeper resistance is real:** MyHeartMap searchers met "blank stares, red tape, or mistrust" in buildings [snippet] — [Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/uncategorized/myheartmap-challenge-ends-more-than-1100-aeds-found/).
- **Polish-specific constraint from the organiser's mentor:** only a provider (institution or company) that takes responsibility for the dataset can publish on dane.gov.pl; the portal's mechanics cannot be rebuilt so that anyone publishes; the stated problem is that data are "rozproszone i niezweryfikowane (nikt nie bierze za nie odpowiedzialności)"; the logo and slogan are for the promotional action aimed at potential data providers, not for pitching the solution [read] — local file `docs/OdMentora.md`.
- **Polish accessibility problem that messaging can use:** the task statement cites a 3 March case in central Warsaw where AEDs were reportedly behind locked doors [read] — local file `docs/MC DZD - Pełna treść zadania konkursowego-1.pdf`; a March 2026 Sejm interpellation (no. 16205) raises AEDs kept in locked cabinets, civil-defence stores and offices [snippet] — [Sejm API](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/16205/body).

### Inferences
- Because the Polish target is "take responsibility for a dataset", the campaign's reward should attach to the institution's name: a public list of providers, an embeddable/door badge tied to a published dataset, and a ranking of municipalities or sectors. These mirror Guardian naming, Taiwan's certificate and PulsePoint/Danish rankings; none of these was tested experimentally in the sources found.
- A data-quality or freshness score per provider is the institutional analogue of Défib'Action's "confirm" badge and The Circuit's quarterly reminder: it rewards maintenance, not just the first upload.
- Offer a delegation path (facility managers, AED service companies, chains uploading one file for all sites), as France allows; multi-site owners are the highest-yield targets (one BHF Guardian manages 182 devices).
- Messaging should lead with visibility and time ("niewidoczny AED nikogo nie uratuje"-type framing) and reassure about effort; liability arguments are absent from the successful campaigns reviewed.
- The slogan can safely make a quantified promise only if the tool really delivers it (e.g. template plus single-file upload), since "5 minutes" is the benchmark set abroad.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study was found that isolates the effect of badges, leaderboards, certificates or reminders on AED registration by institutions; the evidence above is programme self-reporting.
- No evidence found (either way) on fear-of-liability messaging for AED owners, or on embeddable web badges specifically.
- No quantitative evaluation of Taiwan's certification on registration completeness was found.
- The BHF organisation-admin guide and Circuit bulk-upload features were not read; details of bulk registration are unverified.

## 4. Visual standards: ILCOR/ISO AED sign, colour conventions, Polish government identity, accessibility

### Takeaway
Green square + white heart + lightning flash (+ white cross top right) is the standardised wayfinding sign for an AED (ISO 7010 E010 / ILCOR), so a campaign logo should echo its vocabulary without reproducing or altering the safety sign itself; and because the action would be run by a ministry, the logo must sit beside, not incorporate, the state eagle mark.

### Cited Findings
- **ILCOR sign rules (2008 letter):** "Sign must be a green square without letters"; "This safety sign requires a cross in the right upper corner"; "Heart and flash are standard symbols"; "Text is allowed in a separate box"; designed per ISO 7010 with colours/symbols per ISO 3864-3; ILCOR asks manufacturers, public organisations and governments to adopt it [snippet] — [ILCOR letter](https://www.ilcor.org/data/letter-ILCOR-AED-sign.pdf); [ANZCOR copy](https://resus.org.au/?p=4200).
- **ISO 7010:** E010 is the registered AED pictogram; safe-condition signs (safety equipment, exits) are green and square or rectangular [snippet] — [Wikipedia, ISO 7010](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_7010); the older ILCOR SVG is marked superseded by `ISO 7010 E010.svg` on the OSM wiki [snippet] — [OSM wiki file](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/File:ILCOR_AED_sign.svg).
- **The standard sign is poorly understood:** in a 2024 study only 187 participants (20%) correctly identified the ISO AED sign [snippet] — [Resuscitation Plus, Dec 2024 (DOAJ)](https://doaj.org/article/42e6a62b0c174571a381ed5ac71a01f3).
- **UK redesign precedent:** a July 2015 survey of 1,895 adults found under half understood the 2006 UK sign; after a second survey (2,115 responses, Dec 2015) the lightning bolt was replaced by a stylised ECG trace, the wording became "Defibrillator – Heart Restarter", and a supine figure showing pad placement was added; commercial bulk producers must ask BHF for permission and files [read] — [RCUK, standard sign guidance](https://www.resus.org.uk/library/additional-guidance/guidance-defibrillators/guidance-standard-sign).
- **Colour practice in the campaigns reviewed:** Staying Alive uses a green heart with white bolt; Défib'Action adds orange as an accent; Géo'DAE uses blue (pin) plus red (ECG line) with national tricolour cue [read, visual observation] — [Staying Alive CP](https://stayingalive.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CP-DefibAction-2026.pdf).
- **Polish government identity:** Council of Ministers regulation of 30 Sep 2022 (Dz.U. 2022 poz. 2102, in force, published 14 Oct 2022; basis art. 10b(4) of the Act on the Council of Ministers) sets a common mark using the eagle image established for the state coat of arms, drawn flat/two-dimensional after Aleksander Bąk's design, with national colours [snippet + read] — [ELI Dz.U. 2022 poz. 2102](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2022/2102/ogl); [gov.pl, wspólna identyfikacja wizualna](https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/wspolna-identyfikacja-wizualna-administracji-rzadowej).
  - Users: the Prime Minister, members of the Council of Ministers, the Head of KPRM, ministries and KPRM (mandatory); heads of central offices may adopt it voluntarily [snippet] — [TVN24](https://tvn24.pl/polska/nowe-godlo-w-administracji-rzadowej-komentarz-centrum-informacyjnego-rzadu-st6171352).
  - Scope includes electronic documents, websites, letters, social media and information/promotional materials; § 4(2) allows the mark without the eagle in specified cases [snippet] — [Lege Artis commentary](https://czasopismo.legeartis.org/2022/10/wspolna-identyfikacja-wizualna-organow-administracji-rzadowej-rozporzadzenie/).
  - Ministries moved from their own logos to the common mark [read] — [gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/wspolna-identyfikacja-wizualna-administracji-rzadowej).
- **Typical parameters in Polish public-sector brand books (for calibration):**
  - Clear space defined as a multiple of a construction module (e.g. five modules all round) and "no objects that are not part of the mark" inside it [snippet] — [Instytut Książki brand book](https://www.instytutksiazki.pl/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/kz-dziennik-literacki-1.pdf); [gov.pl ministerial brand book](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/c216bcf6-fe56-45f8-b50e-f0e3aeef6343).
  - Minimum size stated separately for print and screen, e.g. 16 mm print / 40 px digital height, 50 px for a simplified variant, 11 mm (Centrum e-Zdrowia), 10 mm and 8 mm for logotype/signet (GDOŚ) [snippet; exact ministry-to-file mapping not verified] — [CeZ brand book](https://cez.gov.pl/sites/default/files/paragraph.attachments.field_attachments/2022-02/zalacznik_nr_3_brand_book_60f982bc59627.pdf); [gov.pl attachment](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/f498e337-27e2-4d72-ac94-c8b301283b42).
  - Colours given in CMYK, Pantone, RGB and HEX (example red CMYK 0/100/100/0, Pantone 2347 C; black CMYK 0/0/0/100, Pantone Black C); some add RAL [snippet] — [UPRP brand book](https://uprp.gov.pl/sites/default/files/inline-files/Ksi%C4%99ga%20znaku%20Urz%C4%99du%20Patentowego%20RP.pdf); [gov.pl attachment](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/c216bcf6-fe56-45f8-b50e-f0e3aeef6343).
- **Accessibility requirement in the brief:** the solution must comply with the current WCAG version and be responsive [read] — local task PDF.

### Inferences
- Do not use the eagle, the red-white flag shapes or anything resembling the ministerial mark inside the campaign logo; design the logo as a stand-alone sign with a clear-space rule generous enough to be lined up next to the Ministry of Digital Affairs mark and the dane.gov.pl mark in a partner bar.
- Do not modify ISO 7010 E010 into a logo (a recoloured or decorated safety sign would dilute a wayfinding standard) but do borrow its vocabulary — green field, white heart, bolt or ECG line — so the logo is read as "AED" at a glance. Since only about 20% recognise the bare sign and the UK found an ECG trace and plain words work better, pair the symbol with words; a text-free emblem is risky.
- Green as primary colour signals "safe condition/first aid" and matches AED signage; red reads as emergency/prohibition/fire and also collides with the red of the state mark placed beside it. A data cue (dot grid, pin, braces, table cell) is what distinguishes this action from the many heart-only marks listed in section 3.
- Provide positive, negative (white on green/dark) and one-colour black versions, and specify minimum sizes for both print (mm) and screen (px), as Polish public brand books do.

### Gaps
- The exact ISO 3864 safety-green specification and ISO's own reproduction rules for E010 were not retrieved (ISO text is paywalled); do not quote a HEX value as "the ISO green" without checking.
- The full text and annexes of Dz.U. 2022 poz. 2102 (clear space, minimum size, typeface, colour values of the government mark) and any co-branding rules with campaign logos were not read; no rule on EU funding marks was found this session.
- Whether the ILCOR/ISO sign is subject to licensing limits for use in logos was not established.
- Background knowledge not verified this session (verify before quoting): WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 requires 4.5:1 contrast for normal text and SC 1.4.11 requires 3:1 for meaningful graphical objects, with logotypes formally exempt from 1.4.3 — check at https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/.

## 5. Minimum contents of a professional brand book, and free tools for SVG logo files and a PDF brand book

### Takeaway
The brief's mandatory minimum is four items (colours, clear space, minimum size, prohibited uses) plus a 3–5 sentence concept; Polish public-sector brand books consistently add construction grid, variants and typography, so including those is the cheap way to look professional.

### Cited Findings
- **Mandatory per the brief:** brand book containing "przynajmniej kolorystykę, pole ochronne, minimalną wielkość i niedozwolone wykorzystywanie znaku oraz maksymalnie 3-5 zdań opisu koncepcji"; deliverables: logo as .jpg, .png, .pdf and any open format; brand book as .pdf [read] — local file `docs/MC DZD - Pełna treść zadania konkursowego-1.pdf`.
- **Common structure of Polish public brand books** (same blocks recur across institutions) [snippet]:
  - construction/modular grid — e.g. grid built on one third of module X ([e-czasPL, GUM](https://infokiosk.gum.gov.pl/download/1/9441/KSIEGAZNAKUe-czasPL2021.pdf)); "Logo na siatce modułowej" ([Polski Bon Turystyczny](https://bonturystyczny.polska.travel/download/ksiega_znaku_PBT.pdf));
  - clear space as a module multiple, with a ban on foreign objects inside it ([Instytut Książki](https://www.instytutksiazki.pl/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/kz-dziennik-literacki-1.pdf));
  - minimum size for print and digital; use below the minimum is not allowed ([Trans-Tacho, GUM](https://www.gum.gov.pl/download/1/10974/ksiegaznakuprojektutrans-tacho.pdf));
  - colour values in CMYK, Pantone, RGB, HEX (sometimes RAL) ([UPRP](https://uprp.gov.pl/sites/default/files/inline-files/Ksi%C4%99ga%20znaku%20Urz%C4%99du%20Patentowego%20RP.pdf));
  - colour, achromatic and negative versions (the government regulation itself distinguishes colour and achromatic forms) ([ELI Dz.U. 2022 poz. 2102](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2022/2102/ogl/pol/pdf));
  - typography chapter; list of prohibited/incorrect uses ("Niedozwolone użycie", "Błędne użycie logo", "Formy niedozwolone") ([Polski Bon Turystyczny](https://bonturystyczny.polska.travel/download/ksiega_znaku_PBT.pdf); [BRPD OPZ annex](https://brpd.gov.pl/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Za%c5%82acznik-nr-1-do-OPZ.pdf)).
- **Open-licence typeface seen in a comparable campaign:** Défib'Action's 2026 press material is set in Lexend (PDF metadata) [read] — [Staying Alive CP](https://stayingalive.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CP-DefibAction-2026.pdf).
- **Figma free tier (2026):** Starter plan is free, one team folder limited to 3 Figma Design/Sites files, unlimited drafts, 30-day version history, no Dev Mode; drafts can be shared view-only [snippet] — [Figma Starter plan overview](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/13838684089751-Starter-plan-overview); [Figma pricing](https://www.figma.com/pricing/).

### Inferences
- A hackathon-proof brand book outline (8–10 pages): 1) concept in 3–5 sentences with name and slogan; 2) primary mark and its parts (signet, logotype, slogan lock-up); 3) construction grid; 4) clear space in module units; 5) minimum size in mm and px, plus a small-size/favicon variant; 6) colours with HEX, RGB, CMYK and nearest Pantone, and contrast ratios; 7) variants: colour, negative, monochrome black, monochrome white; 8) typography with an open-licence font; 9) prohibited uses (stretching, recolouring, effects, low-contrast backgrounds, rotation, clear-space violation, altering the safety-sign cue); 10) applications: partner bar next to ministry/dane.gov.pl marks, institutional badge/sticker, social tile, dataset-page banner.
- "Any open format" is satisfied by SVG; also export the PDF as vector. Keep the master as one SVG with text converted to outlines so fonts are not required.
- Rights: the brief requires the team to hold all rights, so use only self-drawn shapes and fonts under the SIL Open Font License; avoid AI-generated marks whose provenance cannot be shown, and state the font licence in the brand book.
- Applications that show the institutional badge in use double as proof of "potencjał promocyjny", a scoring criterion.

### Gaps
- No authoritative standard defining brand-book contents was found; the structure above is induced from Polish public-sector examples (search snippets, files not opened individually).
- Penpot (open-source, browser-based, SVG-native) and Inkscape (free desktop SVG editor) were not verified this session: the tool search returned only Figma results. Whether Figma Starter restricts SVG/PDF export was also not confirmed; sources conflicted on editor limits (unlimited collaborators per a Figma forum reply vs "up to 2 editors" per a third-party page). Check https://penpot.app, https://inkscape.org and https://help.figma.com directly.
- Open-font licence details (e.g. Lexend, Inter, Atkinson Hyperlegible under OFL) not verified this session; confirm on the font's source page.

Hard evidence found is limited to the GOV.UK 2025 brand refresh (a minimal wordmark element, the "dot", extended into a flexible system) and to the campaigns reviewed above, which rely on one simple symbol, a short imperative line and a system of collectible badges; a broader, well-sourced trend survey could not be assembled this session.

### Inferences
- "Modern" for the jury most plausibly means: a flat, geometric mark that survives at 16–24 px (favicon, map pin, badge), one or two colours, no gradients or shading (in line with the flattened state eagle and the GOV.UK dot), and a system rather than a single lock-up — i.e. the same element reused as map pin, provider badge, progress counter and social tile.
- A small motion idea is cheap to show in a pitch and signals currency: e.g. the mark's line element animating as a heartbeat or a dot "appearing on the map"; present it as an optional variant, with the static SVG as the master. No source this session documents motion-logo adoption in public health campaigns.
- The GOV.UK cost controversy is a reminder to frame the Polish identity as low-cost and reusable (open font, SVG, template badge generator), which fits an open-data ministry.
