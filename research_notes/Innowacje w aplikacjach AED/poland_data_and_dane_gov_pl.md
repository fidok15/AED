## (A) Which sources of AED data exist in Poland — coverage, format, licence, freshness, access

### Takeaway
There is no state AED register. The only nationwide, open, machine-readable dataset is OpenStreetMap (served by OpenAEDMap: 18,297 AEDs in Poland on 2026-11-21, ODbL, GeoJSON API) and the state itself already consumes it — 112 operators, the GUGiK Geoportal "Defibrylatory" layer and the map module used by medical dispatchers in SWD PRM all run on OSM data. dane.gov.pl holds only five municipal AED resources (Krosno, Przemyśl, Marki, Legionowo as files = 74 rows in total, plus Warsaw as a link to a key-protected API), each with a different schema.

### Cited Findings

**A1. dane.gov.pl — every AED dataset found (search `q=defibrylator`, `q=AED`, resource search)**

- Dataset search for "defibrylator"/"defibrylatory" returns exactly 4 datasets; resource search returns one more AED resource hidden inside a generic Legionowo "Zdrowie" dataset. No dataset from GUS, a voivode, a ministry or any national body was found. [API] — [datasets?q=defibrylator](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/datasets?q=defibrylator&per_page=100), [resources?q=defibrylator](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/resources?q=defibrylator&per_page=50)

**A2. OpenStreetMap / OpenAEDMap (the de facto national register)**

- `aed.openstreetmap.org.pl` now 308-redirects to `https://openaedmap.org/pl/`. [API] — [aed.openstreetmap.org.pl](https://aed.openstreetmap.org.pl/)
- Count: 18,297 AED features for Poland and 179,672 worldwide on 2026-11-21. [API] — [openaedmap.org/api/v1/countries/names](https://openaedmap.org/api/v1/countries/names)
- Growth: 2,154 AEDs in Poland in February 2022 → 17,508 at end of September 2026 (per a commercial blog citing the OpenAEDMap API). [press] — [cardiomedico.com, 30 Sep 2026](https://cardiomedico.com/Mapa-AED-jak-znalezc-defibrylator-i-jak-zglosic-wlasny-AED-na-mape-blog-pol-1790894995.html)
- Access: per-country GeoJSON at `https://openaedmap.org/api/v1/countries/PL.geojson` (HTTP 200, `Content-Type: application/geo+json`, `Cache-Control: public, max-age=3600`, no key). Each feature is a Point with `@osm_id`, `@osm_type`, `@osm_version` plus raw OSM tags. [API] — [PL.geojson](https://openaedmap.org/api/v1/countries/PL.geojson)
- Data-model completeness in the Polish extract (my count over 18,297 features): `access` 16,917 (92%), `indoor` 15,969 (87%), `defibrillator:location` 12,317 (67%), `opening_hours` 6,781 (37%), `operator` 6,007 (33%), `check_date` 5,981 (33%), `phone` 4,481 (24%), `level` 3,701 (20%), `image` 2,168 (12%); 113 features carry `ref:api-um-warszawa-pl` (i.e. were matched to Warsaw's city API). [API] — [PL.geojson](https://openaedmap.org/api/v1/countries/PL.geojson)

The scheme was validated in the project "Mapa oraz rejestr urządzeń AED na terenie Polski". [doc] — [OSM wiki Pl:Tag:emergency=defibrillator](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Pl:Tag:emergency%3Ddefibrillator)

### Inferences
- Because 112 operators, Geoportal and the dispatchers' UMM already ingest OSM, any AED record that reaches OSM reaches the emergency services; a dane.gov.pl dataset that is not synchronised with OSM would be a parallel silo. Conversely, OSM has no accountable "data provider" — exactly the gap the mentor describes.
- Licence friction: OSM/OpenAEDMap data is ODbL (share-alike). The portal's licence vocabulary contains only CC0 and the CC 4.0 family (see section B), and the provider guide recommends CC0. Republishing an OSM-derived national dataset on dane.gov.pl under CC0/CC BY therefore looks legally problematic; the clean flow is owners → dane.gov.pl (CC0) → OSM, not the reverse. This needs a lawyer's confirmation.
- The five portal resources total 74 downloadable AED rows versus 18,297 in OSM — the portal currently covers ~0.4% of the known national stock.
- Marki copied Krosno's column headers verbatim (an informal "template" already spreads by imitation), yet still broke the file — evidence that a reference schema needs a validator, not just a sample file.
- Przemyśl's flags (dynamic + HVD + research data, "daily" updates on a file dated 2024) and Legionowo's HVD flag show that provider-set metadata on the portal is unverified self-declaration.

### Gaps

## How inconsistent are the sources; how many AEDs are there; how many cardiac arrests

### Takeaway
The existing datasets share no schema, no identifier, no coordinate-order convention and mostly no availability information; even in OSM only 37% of Polish AEDs have opening hours and 33% a check date. No official total exists — the Ministry of Health says it cannot give a number or a "last verified" date; the best counts are 18.3 k (OSM) and "10,000+" (Pierwszy Ratownik). Dispatchers register roughly 3,300 cardiac-arrest calls a month (~40 k a year).

### Cited Findings
- Five dane.gov.pl resources use four different schemas: Krosno/Marki (Polish prose headers, one free-text address), Przemyśl (CMS export with English keys, `geometry`, galleries, a marketing description repeated in every row), Legionowo (normalised address parts + TERC/SIMC codes + `szerokosc_geograficzna`/`dlugosc_geograficzna`), Warsaw (opaque API). None has opening hours, access type, in/outdoor flag, operator contact, device status or verification date. [API] — [Krosno](https://api.dane.gov.pl/media/resources/20240219/Defibrylator_jAmxQcp.csv), [Przemyśl](https://przemysl.skycms.com.pl/open-data/20/1730818801_b50R6UVxPgoVdBbq/csv), [Legionowo data API](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/resources/2625221/data?per_page=2)
- Coordinate order differs: Krosno lists longitude then latitude; Marki uses the same headers but stores latitude then longitude; Legionowo lists latitude then longitude under explicit names; Przemyśl uses GeoJSON `[lon, lat]`. [API] — same files as above
- Separators/encodings differ: comma + UTF-8 (Krosno, Legionowo), semicolon + BOM (Przemyśl), whole-row quoting (Marki). [API] — same files as above
- In OSM (Poland) 63% of AEDs lack `opening_hours`, 67% lack `check_date`, 67% lack `operator`, 33% lack a location description; 632 features carry a `fixme` tag. [API] — [PL.geojson](https://openaedmap.org/api/v1/countries/PL.geojson)
- Ministry of Health, 13 Nov 2026: "Nie ma zatem podstaw do wskazania jednej daty „ostatniej weryfikacji” wszystkich AED w Polsce. Poszczególne lokalne wykazy mogą być aktualizowane niezależnie"; current rules "nie określają jednolitego, obligatoryjnego zakresu danych o AED obejmującego m.in. dokładną lokalizację, godziny dostępności, ograniczenia dostępu oraz podmiot odpowiedzialny". [doc] — [MZ reply to interpellation 20763](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/attachment/ATTDZYND5/i20763-o1.pdf)
- Same reply: for dispatchers "szczególnie istotna jest nie tylko sama lokalizacja urządzenia, lecz również wiarygodna informacja o jego faktycznej dostępności w danym czasie", and responsibility for keeping it current "powinna pozostawać powiązana z … właścicielem lub zarządcą". [doc] — [MZ reply](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/attachment/ATTDZYND5/i20763-o1.pdf)
- Interpellation 20763 itself describes the problem as data "rozproszone pomiędzy różnymi systemami", with currency depending on individual owners, database admins and volunteers, and no mechanism for reporting installation, failure, relocation or withdrawal. [doc] — [interpellation 20763](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/20763/body)
- Real-world consequence: the AED nearest to Plac Zamkowy (Royal Castle guardhouse) is available only 8:00–18:00; on the evening of 2 March 2026 it could not be reached (see section C). [press] — [RMF24/PAP](https://www.rmf24.pl/regiony/warszawa/news-przybywa-przenosnych-defibrylatorow-w-warszawie-oto-miejsca-,nId,8069530)
- Totals available: 18,297 (OSM, 2026-11-21) [API] — [OpenAEDMap](https://openaedmap.org/api/v1/countries/names); "10 000+" (Pierwszy Ratownik) [doc] — [pierwszyratownik.pl](https://pierwszyratownik.pl/); 1,605 known in 2016 [press] — [mp.pl](https://www.mp.pl/ratownictwo/aktualnosci/153234,kiedy-serce-przestaje-bic).
- Cardiac arrests: according to KCMRM, dispatchers took 3,298 calls for sudden cardiac arrest in September 2026 alone (>100 per day, ≈40 k per year); EuReCa-THREE is cited for 6.8% survival to hospital discharge after out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in Poland. [snippet — PAP page body could not be retrieved] — [PAP MediaRoom](https://www.pap.pl/mediaroom/prawie-20-organizacji-ponad-podzialami-w-krakowie-zostanie-podpisany-list-intencyjny)
- European benchmarks quoted by the Polish Resuscitation Council (ERC 2021 epidemiology): bystander AED use on average 28% (range 3.8–59%), survival to discharge on average 8% (0–18%). [snippet] — [prc.krakow.pl Epidemiologia PDF](https://www.prc.krakow.pl/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Epidemiologia.pdf)
- The commonly repeated claim that an AED used within 3–5 minutes raises survival "nawet do 70%" appears in the interpellation without a source. [doc] — [interpellation 16205](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/16205/body)

### Inferences
- OSM (18.3 k) and Pierwszy Ratownik (10 k+) overlap to an unknown degree; nobody has published a de-duplicated national figure, so "about 18 thousand mapped, true number unknown" is the defensible statement.
- The 3,298 figure counts dispatcher calls, not confirmed arrests, and the annualisation is a simple ×12.
- The three fields the Ministry of Health itself names as missing (availability hours, access restrictions, responsible entity) plus a verification date are the minimum a reference schema must add beyond location.

### Gaps
- No Poland-specific bystander-CPR rate or bystander-AED-use rate found in a primary source.
- No measured duplicate rate between sources (would require matching OSM vs. municipal lists vs. Pierwszy Ratownik).
- The 6.8% survival figure and the KCMRM count need confirmation in the primary publications.

## (B) How dane.gov.pl works for data providers — organisation, formats, openness score, metadata, licences, API, harvester, validation, map, standards, source code

### Takeaway
Only an institution with a provider profile (created by portal admins on e-mail request) can publish; it does so manually in the admin panel or automatically through one of three harvesters (CKAN, XML, DCAT-AP). A clean single-sheet CSV/XLSX stored in the portal becomes a queryable table with its own JSON:API and is auto-converted to JSON-LD, which lifts it to ★4; the map is **not** triggered by column names — an editor must tag columns as longitude / latitude / label (WGS84, numeric) in the "Mapy i wykresy" tab. The Ministry already runs two "reference schema + XML template + instructions" programmes (developers' flat prices, found property) that are a ready pattern for AED.

**B4. Formats and the 5-star openness score**

- Standard techniczny (2020 edition; the current binding version is Annex 2 to the Programme for Opening Data 2021–2027, Resolution no. 28 of the Council of Ministers of 18 Feb 2021): ★1 any format without licence restrictions (PDF, JPG, unstructured TXT) — "Nie zaleca się publikowania danych na pierwszym poziomie otwartości"; ★2 structured but proprietary (XLS); ★3 open structured formats (CSV, JSON, XML; vector spatial data GML, SHP, KML, GeoJSON, GPX); ★4 URIs/RDF (RDF/XML, Turtle, N3, JSON-LD, N-Triples, RDFa); ★5 linked to other data. XLSX/DOCX "nie są zalecane … powyżej poziomu 2". Data at ★3 and above should preferably be served through an API. [doc] — [Standard techniczny 2020 (PDF)](https://dane.gov.pl/media/ckeditor/2020/06/16/standard-techniczny.pdf), [Program otwierania danych 2021–2027 page](https://dane.gov.pl/pl/knowledgebase/useful-materials/program-otwierania-danych-na-lata-2021-2027)
- CSV rules: RFC 4180 recommended at ★3 and required at ★4 — CRLF line ends, comma separator, double quotes around special characters, UTF-8 (or correct `Content-type`); exactly one header row; no empty or duplicate headers; same number of cells in every row; no empty rows/columns; each row independent (no blanks standing for "same as above"). Dates/times in ISO 8601; decimal point, no thousands separators. [doc] — [Standard techniczny 2020, ch. 3–4](https://dane.gov.pl/media/ckeditor/2020/06/16/standard-techniczny.pdf)
- Spreadsheet rules restated in 2026 for a thematic programme: one header row; no merged cells; one record per row; no aggregate formulas or filters; nothing outside the table; no external formulas; homogeneous column types; one elementary value per column; single sheet; **empty cells must be filled with the conventional sign "X"**; dates `yyyy-mm-dd`, date-times `yyyy-mm-ddThh:mm` or `yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm:ss`. [doc] — [Wskazówki dot. przygotowania danych v1.0 (15 May 2026)](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/278/Wskaz%C3%B3wki_dotycz%C4%85ce_przygotowania_danych_do_publikacji_w_portalu_v1.0.pdf)
- Special signs vocabulary: `-` phenomenon did not occur; `.` no (reliable) information; `X` filling in impossible or pointless; `:` no information (Eurostat); `#` statistical confidentiality. [doc] — [XSD](https://www.dane.gov.pl/static/xml/otwarte_dane_latest.xsd)
- PDF is allowed only as a supplementary document next to machine-readable files or an API. [doc] — [FAQ](https://dane.gov.pl/pl/knowledgebase/useful-materials/faq)
- How the software scores: each recognised extension has a default score in `SUPPORTED_CONTENT_TYPES` — csv/tsv/json/xml/geojson/gml/gpx/kml/kmz/shp/ods/dbf/html = 3; xls/xlsx = 2; pdf/doc/docx/jpg/png/txt/ppt = 1; jsonld/rdf/ttl/n3/nt/nq/trig/trix = 4; JSON, XML and RDF files go through content calculators (an RDF graph that links to more than one domain counts as linked data); password-protected archive = 0; multi-file archive = 1; single-file archive scored as the inner file. [code] — [mcod/settings/base.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/settings/base.py), [score_calculation.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/score_computation/score_calculation.py), [contains_linked_data.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/score_computation/calculators/contains_linked_data.py)
- Automatic uplift: the portal converts xls/xlsx → CSV + JSON-LD and CSV → JSON-LD, but only for files stored in the portal (not remote ones) for which a tabular view was generated; only the first sheet is converted. [doc] — [Przewodnik v1.23, ch. 5.7](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/283/Przewodnik_dla_dostawc%C3%B3w_danych_1.23.pdf); implementation in `increase_openness_score()` / `convert_csv_to_jsonld()` (CSVW-based) [code] — [mcod/resources/models.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/models.py)
- Observed effect on AED resources: portal-stored, valid CSV/XLSX = ★4 (Krosno CSV, Marki XLSX, Legionowo CSV); malformed CSV = ★3 (Marki); remote CSV/JSON/GeoJSON = ★3 (Przemyśl); link to an API page = ★3 (Warsaw). [API] — [resources of 3624](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/datasets/3624/resources), [4955](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/datasets/4955/resources), [4581](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/datasets/4581/resources)
- The regulation itself defines the openness level as a portal-computed metadata element ("klasyfikacja ISP na podstawie 5-stopniowej skali, która określa jakość danych"). [doc] — [Rozporządzenie §7 ust. 3 pkt 6](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/116/Rozporz%C4%85dzenie_RM_w_sprawie_portalu_danych.pdf)

**B5. Validation of tabular resources**

- Three validation steps on every upload/link: link validation (reachable), file validation (format recognised), data validation (can a tabular view be built — merged cells, date formats etc.). [code] — [indexed_data.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/indexed_data.py), [goodtables_checks.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/goodtables_checks.py), [Pipfile](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/Pipfile)

[doc] — [Przewodnik v1.23, ch. 12](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/283/Przewodnik_dla_dostawc%C3%B3w_danych_1.23.pdf)

**B6. Map visualisation — exact trigger conditions**

- "Pliki w formacie shp (shapefile) są wizualizowane automatycznie po pozytywnym zakończeniu procesu ich walidacji. Pozostałe pliki można wizualizować z wykorzystaniem zakładki „Mapy i wykresy”" — available only for files that have a tabular view. The editor picks columns in the "Mapa" row and presses "Zapisz definicję mapy". [doc] — [Przewodnik v1.23, ch. 10](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/283/Przewodnik_dla_dostawc%C3%B3w_danych_1.23.pdf)
- Three alternative "map data sets" (exactly one may be used):
  1. geographical coordinates — columns tagged `l` (długość geograficzna / longitude), `b` (szerokość geograficzna / latitude) and `label` (etykieta); all three mandatory; processed as WGS84; both coordinate columns must be of numeric type (`integer` or `number`);
  2. universal address — `uaddress` + `label` (a `|`- or `;`-separated string: postal code without dash, TERC, two SIMC ids, ULIC id, geodetic x/y in metres [EPSG:2180], house number);
  3. address — `place` (miejscowość) + `postal_code` + `label` mandatory, `street` and `house_number` optional; the portal geocodes these through an internal geocoder.
  [doc + code] — [Przewodnik v1.23, ch. 10](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/283/Przewodnik_dla_dostawc%C3%B3w_danych_1.23.pdf), `GEO_TYPES` in [base.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/settings/base.py), `MapsJSONField` in [forms.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/forms.py), [geo.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/geo.py)
- Validation errors enforced by the form: an element chosen more than once; elements from different sets mixed; incomplete set; "Longitude should be a number" / "Latitude should be a number". [code] — [forms.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/forms.py)
- Indexing rule: a resource "has geo data" only if the stored schema has `geo.label` and one of (`uaddress`) / (`b` and `l`) / (`place` and `postal_code`); each row then gets `shape = {"type":"Point","coordinates":[l, b]}` (longitude first) and `label`. [code] — [indexed_data.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/indexed_data.py)
- Confirmed on live data: Krosno (map defined) exposes `visualization_types: ["map","table"]`; Legionowo has clean `szerokosc_geograficzna` / `dlugosc_geograficzna` columns but only `["table"]` — column names alone do not create a map. [API] — [resource 54632](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/resources/54632), [resource 2625221](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/resources/2625221)
- File-level geo recognition (affects format, not the map): GeoJSON detected by content (`FeatureCollection`+`features`, etc.), GPX validated against GPX 1.0/1.1 XSD, GML by `.gml` extension, KML validated against OGC KML 2.2 XSD, GeoTIFF, shapefile archives (`.shp`+`.shx`+`.dbf`, reprojected to EPSG:4326 from the `.prj`). [code] — [geo.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/resources/geo.py)
- "Lokalizacja danych" is a separate, coarse metadata field (territorial units chosen from a list; in XML: up to 20 `terytIdent` codes of 2–7 characters) that powers search-by-region, not point maps. [doc] — [Przewodnik v1.23, ch. 5.2.13](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/283/Przewodnik_dla_dostawc%C3%B3w_danych_1.23.pdf), [XSD](https://www.dane.gov.pl/static/xml/otwarte_dane_latest.xsd)

[doc + code] — [XML guide](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/225/Przewodnik_automatycznego_zasilania_danych_xml.PDF), [mcod/harvester/utils.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/harvester/utils.py)
- Current schema: namespace `urn:otwarte-dane:harvester:1.13` (XSD files 1.0-rc1 … 1.13 ship in the source). Root `<datasets>` → `<dataset status="published|draft">` with `extIdent` (≤36 chars, required), optional `intIdent`, `title{polish,english}` (≤300), `description{polish,english}` (≤1000), `url`, `updateFrequency`, `categories/category`, `conditions`, `resources/resource`, `tags/tag[@lang]`, `lastUpdateDate`, `hasDynamicData`, `hasHighValueData`, `hasHighValueDataFromEuropeanCommissionList`, `hasResearchData`, `supplements` (≤10). `<resource>`: `extIdent`, `url` (must match `https://.*`), `title`, `description`, `availability` (`local` = file copied into the portal, `remote` = served from the provider), `dataDate`, `lastUpdateDate`, the four `has…` flags, `containsProtectedData`, `specialSigns`, `supplements`, `isAutoDataDate`, `endlessDataDateUpdate`, `dataDateUpdatePeriod` (`daily|weekly|monthly`), `autoDataDateStart/End`, `regions/terytIdent`. [doc] — [otwarte_dane_latest.xsd](https://www.dane.gov.pl/static/xml/otwarte_dane_latest.xsd)
- Identity semantics: objects are matched by `extIdent`; changing it deletes the old object (to trash) and creates a new one; objects whose `extIdent` disappears from the XML are removed from the portal; `intIdent` (portal numeric id) lets a provider take over a manually created object and has priority over `extIdent`; with `availability=remote` the provider just updates the file at the URL, with `local` a new `extIdent` is needed to swap the file. [doc] — [XML guide](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/225/Przewodnik_automatycznego_zasilania_danych_xml.PDF)
- Evidence the XML channel is used at scale by private companies: many developer datasets show `source.type = "xml"` with daily imports from the company's own domain (e.g. `https://widawa.com/wp-content/uploads/export/otwarte_dane.xml`, last import 2026-11-21). [API] — [datasets?q=AED (noise results show the pattern)](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/datasets?q=AED&per_page=100)
- **CKAN harvester**: needs a portal URL and an API URL returning `application/json`; consumes the CKAN action `current_package_list_with_resources` (test fixture taken from otwartedane.gdynia.pl); validates licence id against the whitelist and HVD/EC-list flag conflicts. Live examples: Warsaw (`https://dane.um.warszawa.pl/`) and Lublin (`https://otwartedane.lublin.eu/`), both daily. [code + API] — [harvester/models.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/harvester/models.py), [dataset 39512](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/datasets/39512)
- **DCAT-AP harvester**: configured with an API URL and a SPARQL query; fetches through `SPARQLStore(query_endpoint=api_url, returnFormat="application/rdf+xml")` — i.e. it needs a SPARQL endpoint, not a static RDF dump. [code] — [harvester/utils.py](https://github.com/olekstomek/mcod-backend-dane.gov.pl/blob/backend_2.62.3/mcod/harvester/utils.py)

### Inferences
- The lowest-friction compliant deliverable for any AED owner is a single-sheet XLSX or RFC 4180 CSV (UTF-8, comma, one header row, ISO dates, decimal point, "X" in empty cells) with **separate numeric latitude and longitude columns in WGS84 plus one human-readable label column**; uploaded as a portal-stored file it yields table view + data API + automatic JSON-LD (★4), and one manual step in "Mapy i wykresy" yields the map.
- The XML harvester schema (v1.13) has no element for the map definition or column types, so a harvested AED file gets a map only after an editor sets it once in the admin panel (assumption: the definition persists across re-imports with the same `extIdent` — not verified).
- `availability=remote` keeps the file on the provider's server and, per the guide, remote files are not auto-converted to CSV/JSON-LD → they stay at ★3. For ★4 a harvested AED file should use `availability=local`.
- A hackathon tool can prove "dane.gov.pl compatibility" offline by (a) validating its generated XML against `otwarte_dane_latest.xsd` and producing the lowercase `.md5`, (b) running the same goodtables checks on its CSV/XLSX, and (c) emitting the JSON:API/`colN` shape or simply reading existing AED resources through `/resources/{id}/data`.
- The found-property and developer programmes show the Ministry's own recipe: legal hook → reference data scope (xlsx/csv) → XML template with fixed titles, tags and category → short instructions → one mailbox. An AED proposal shaped the same way would look native; what AED lacks is the legal hook (see section C).
- Because the DCAT harvester requires a SPARQL endpoint, the XML channel (static file + md5 on any HTTPS host) is the realistic automation route for small owners, and CKAN for cities that already run a CKAN portal.

## The open-data law — who must publish and can private entities publish on dane.gov.pl

### Takeaway
Public-sector bodies listed in art. 3 pkt 1–4 of the Act of 11 August 2021 are obliged to publish in the portal; private entities (companies, NGOs, shopping centres) are not obliged but may open a provider profile and publish "private data" if it is of particular importance for innovation or the information society and meets the openness standards — the portal FAQ says so explicitly.

### Cited Findings
- Act: ustawa z dnia 11 sierpnia 2021 r. o otwartych danych i ponownym wykorzystywaniu informacji sektora publicznego (Dz.U. 2021 poz. 1641), implementing Directive (EU) 2019/1024; implementing regulation on the portal: Dz.U. 2022 poz. 2415. [doc] — [Prawo (knowledge base)](https://dane.gov.pl/pl/knowledgebase/useful-materials/prawo), [Act text](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2021/1641/text.html)
- Obliged entities (art. 3): pkt 1 public finance sector units; pkt 2 other state organisational units without legal personality; pkt 3 legal persons created to meet needs of a general character (bodies governed by public law); pkt 4 associations of those; pkt 5 other entities (certain sectoral entities / public-service operators) under listed conditions. [doc, via automated extraction] — [Act text](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2021/1641/text.html)
- "Lista podmiotów zobowiązanych do publikacji danych w portalu znajduje się w art. 3 pkt 1-4 ustawy". [doc] — [FAQ](https://dane.gov.pl/pl/knowledgebase/useful-materials/faq)
- Private data is defined (art. 2 pkt 5) as content held and produced by an entity other than an obliged entity, excluding personal data. Art. 35 ust. 1: "W portalu danych mogą być udostępniane dane prywatne o szczególnym znaczeniu dla rozwoju innowacyjności w państwie lub rozwoju społeczeństwa informacyjnego". Art. 36: the entity publishing private data consents to its use by portal users and may set conditions (attribution/source information, liability limits). [doc, via automated extraction] — [Act text](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2021/1641/text.html), [lexlege art. 35](https://lexlege.pl/ponowne-wykorz-inf-sekt-publ/art-35/)
- FAQ: "Prowadzę firmę, czy mogę założyć w portalu profil dostawcy i publikować w nim dane? Tak, pod warunkiem, że planowane do udostępnienia dane są danymi o szczególnym znaczeniu dla rozwoju innowacyjności w państwie lub rozwoju społeczeństwa informacyjnego oraz zostały przygotowane zgodnie ze standardami otwartości danych". [doc] — [FAQ](https://dane.gov.pl/pl/knowledgebase/useful-materials/faq)
- Art. 10: obliged entities share information "jeżeli to możliwe" as open data, in a machine-readable format with metadata. Open data is defined as data in an open, non-proprietary machine-readable format. [doc, via automated extraction] — [Act text](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2021/1641/text.html)
- Art. 33: the Council of Ministers may, by regulation, define the scope of public-sector information to be published in the portal and the schedule for publishing and updating it. The knowledge base has a page "Harmonogramy udostępniania danych". [doc, via automated extraction; page title via CMS API] — [Act text](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2021/1641/text.html), [CMS tree](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/api/pages/knowledgebase/useful-materials/)
- Private companies already publish at scale: thousands of developer datasets, each under the company's own provider profile, many fed by XML. [API] — [stats](https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/stats)

### Inferences
- A municipality, hospital, school, public transport operator or state agency that owns AEDs is an obliged entity, and a list of its AEDs is public-sector information it may already publish today under its own profile — no new law is needed for that, only willingness and a schema.
- For private AED owners the legal gate is the "particular importance" test of art. 35; life-saving infrastructure location data is an easy case to argue, but the portal administrator decides on creating the profile.
- Because every dataset must belong to a provider profile, "one nationwide dataset" realistically means either (a) one national custodian institution that aggregates and takes responsibility, or (b) many provider-owned datasets in one enforced schema that are trivially mergeable through the portal API — the developer-prices model is (b).
- Art. 33 gives the government a regulation-level tool to schedule AED lists for publication by public bodies without amending the emergency-medical-services act (it would not bind private owners).

### Gaps
- Exact wording of art. 32 (duty to publish in the portal) and art. 35 ust. 2 cross-references was obtained through automated extraction and should be re-read in the consolidated text (Dz.U. 2023 poz. 1524 according to search results — not opened).
- No example found of an NGO or shopping-centre operator with a provider profile publishing safety-equipment data.
- How the portal administrator applies the art. 35 test in practice (criteria, refusal cases) is undocumented.

## (C) Law — is there any obligation to register an AED; planned registry; the March 2026 death; signage; good-Samaritan rules

### Takeaway
As of 13 November 2026 the Ministry of Health confirms in writing that there is no obligation to report an AED, no central register, no mandatory data scope, no national availability classification, and that it has conducted no formal legislative work on any of these. Three interpellations in 2026 raised the issue (to MSWiA, the Minister of Digital Affairs and the Minister of Health); ministries point to OpenStreetMap-based layers and to owners' responsibility. The photographer's death on 2 March 2026 happened at Plac Zamkowy in Warsaw (not inside the Sejm) and no legislative announcement following it could be found.

### Inferences
- The challenge's premise matches the official record: the state relies on a volunteer-maintained map and openly says nobody is formally responsible for its currency. The Ministry of Health's own formulation — responsibility should rest with "właściciel lub zarządca" — maps exactly onto dane.gov.pl's provider model (the owner institution as accountable data provider).
- With no legislative work under way, a voluntary reference schema published by the Ministry of Digital Affairs (as it did for found property and flat prices) is the only instrument available in the short term; it can later be given teeth by an art. 33 regulation (public bodies) or a PRM Act amendment (all owners).
- Poland has no explicit "good Samaritan" statute for AED use; protection derives from art. 5 PRM Act and general criminal-law rules, and professional bodies are asking for a clearer clause.
- The framing "death of a Sejm photographer" is accurate about the person's work but the incident location was Plac Zamkowy; presentations should say "long-time Sejm photojournalist who died at Plac Zamkowy" to avoid a factual slip.

## Accessibility and "native" look — WCAG version in force and gov.pl design system

### Takeaway
Polish public-sector websites and apps fall under the Act of 4 April 2019 on digital accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA). The European standard EN 301 549 v4.1.1 (September 2026) moves to WCAG 2.2 AA, but no Polish amendment adopting it was found, so WCAG 2.1 AA is the legal floor and WCAG 2.2 AA the safe target. A "Design System GOV.PL" exists by name, but I could not reach a public component library; the dane.gov.pl front-end code itself (Angular 18 + Bootstrap 5 + Leaflet, GPL v3) is downloadable.

### Cited Findings
- The provider guide reminds providers that the Act of 4 April 2019 on digital accessibility of websites and mobile applications of public entities applies, that dane.gov.pl publishes an accessibility declaration, and asks providers to follow WCAG when preparing data and descriptions (e.g. declare English text in descriptions). [doc] — [Przewodnik v1.23, ch. 14](https://cms.dane.gov.pl/documents/283/Przewodnik_dla_dostawc%C3%B3w_danych_1.23.pdf)
- The 2019 Act requires WCAG 2.1; government accessibility materials are at gov.pl/web/dostepnosc-cyfrowa. [snippet] — [gov.pl/web/dostepnosc-cyfrowa](https://www.gov.pl/web/dostepnosc-cyfrowa)
- EN 301 549 V4.1.1 was published on 2 September 2026; V3.2.1 referenced WCAG 2.1 A/AA, V4.1.1 references WCAG 2.2 A/AA. [snippet] — [nda.ie](https://nda.ie/news/en301549-published), [deque.com](https://www.deque.com/blog/en-301-549-v4-1-1-is-final-what-changed-what-it-means-and-what-you-should-do/), [European Commission: latest changes to the accessibility standard](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/pl/policies/latest-changes-accessibility-standard)
- Until V4.1.1 is cited in the Official Journal of the EU, V3.2.1 (WCAG 2.1 AA) remains the basis for presumption of conformity. [snippet, commercial source] — [browserstack.com](https://www.browserstack.com/blog/what-en-301-549-v4-1-1-means-for-your-accessibility-strategy/)
- The private-sector accessibility act transposing the European Accessibility Act applies from 28 June 2025. [snippet] — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/standard-wcag-2-2-od-kiedy-obowiazuje-i-jakie-sa-jego-wytyczne/)
- A Laboratorium Otwartych Danych report gives recommendations on preparing accessible content for reuse on dane.gov.pl. [doc — referenced in the guide; report itself not opened] — [Raporty LOD](https://dane.gov.pl/pl/knowledgebase/useful-materials/raporty-laboratorium-otwartych-danych)
- "Serwis GOV.PL pozwala tworzyć odrębne portale internetowe dla instytucji publicznych przy zachowaniu wytycznych Design Systemu GOV.PL." [doc] — [gov.pl Portal Interoperacyjności i Architektury: Bloki budowlane rozwiązań](https://www.gov.pl/web/ia/bloki-budowlane-rozwiazan)
- dane.gov.pl front-end dependencies: `@angular/core ^18.2.13`, `bootstrap ^5.3.3`, `leaflet ^1.9.4`, `chart.js ^4.4.7`, `@ngx-translate/core ^16`, `ngx-bootstrap ^18.1.3`; full source under GPL v3. [code] — [frontend_latest.zip](https://dane.gov.pl/source-code/frontend_latest.zip), [LICENCE.txt](https://dane.gov.pl/source-code/LICENCE.txt)

### Inferences
- Building the prototype to WCAG 2.2 AA satisfies both today's Polish requirement (2.1 AA) and the incoming European baseline; a map-centric tool needs a non-map alternative (searchable list/table) to meet it — which is exactly what the portal's own tabular view provides.
- The quickest way to look native to the *open-data portal* (rather than to gov.pl generally) is to reuse Bootstrap 5 with the portal's styles and Leaflet, mirroring its table / map / chart tabs; any reuse of portal code makes the derivative GPL v3.
